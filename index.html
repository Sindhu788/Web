Our Story

A Legacy Written in Fire

Zaiqa Royale begins where every great Pakistani meal begins: with patience. Coals are lit hours before the first guest arrives, spice blends are ground in small batches, and marinades rest overnight until the meat surrenders to them completely.

Our kitchen draws on the grill masters of Peshawar, the karahi houses of Lahore and the courtly Mughlai kitchens that shaped the subcontinent's table. Nothing is hurried. Nothing is hidden. What reaches your table is the work of unhurried hands and honest fire.

Hospitality, for us, is an inheritance. You arrive as a guest and are served as family.

The Zaiqa Experience

Three Chapters, One Table

Chapter I

The Fire

Natural charcoal, tandoor walls seasoned over years, and a grill team that reads the flame the way a musician reads a score. Smoke here is an ingredient, never an accident.

Chapter II

The Craft

Before service, our chefs hand-grind spices, tenderise and rest the meat, and taste every pot. Rituals refined over years, repeated daily without shortcuts.

Chapter III

The Heritage

Saffron, cardamom, slow-cooked gravies and warm service shaped by Mughlai court tradition. Pakistani ingredients and generosity, set in a room built for lingering.
